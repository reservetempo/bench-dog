Pages reference their assets by **absolute paths** (`/gates/style.css`), because relative paths break when the page is served without a trailing slash. Don't touch the rest of the site.
- `/gates` is the design doc "Lords & Logicians" for a 2.5D game where logic gates are medieval people. **World rule:** signals are torchbearers (messengers) walking the roads between gates. **Lit torch = 1, dark = 0.** The doc's cast: **Hob Wickfoot**, the torchbearer (small round peasant, patched red hood, torch on a long pole), and **Lord Gainsay**, Baron of Contrary Keep, the **NOT** gate (tall and gaunt, black cloak falling in a hard triangle, round white ruff at the throat, so he wears the NOT symbol; pale face, pointed beard, a long snuffer in one hand and a flint in the other; colors black, rubric-red lining, cold silver). He snuffs a lit torch and, out of spite, lights a dark one. Read their sheets (`id="torchbearer"` and `id="not"` in `public/gates/index.html`) with targeted greps, not whole-file reads.
- **New: a playable proof of concept at `/gates/play`** (user's choices: Three.js, low-poly chess-piece characters, "pick the torch, then send", a beacon at the road's end).
  - Files in `public/gates/play/`: `index.html`, `style.css`, and ES modules `main.js` (entry: renderer, scene, loop, wiring), `map.js` (board data and tiles), `pieces.js` (characters, torches, beacon), `run.js` (the walk and the gate's moment). Rewrite `{ source: '/gates/play', destination: '/gates/play/index.html' }` in `next.config.js`. No link from the homepage or from `/gates` for now (reachable by URL only).
  - **No build step.** Three.js loaded with an import map pinned to `three@0.170.0` on jsDelivr: `"three": "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/build/three.module.min.js"` and, only if an addon is needed, `"three/addons/": "https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/examples/jsm/"`. Don't add npm dependencies.
  - **2.5D view:** fixed `OrthographicCamera` at the classic isometric angle (45° around, about 35° down) looking at the board centre, refit on resize so the whole board fills the canvas at any size, phone width included. No orbit controls.
  - **Look:** dusk/night so torches matter: a cool moonlight directional light (soft shadows) plus a low hemisphere light, a deep night-blue background (`#1f2a4d`-ish). Colors from the palette tokens in `public/gates/style.css` (`--flame-core`, `--flame`, `--flame-edge`, `--rubric`, `--gold`, `--lapis`, `--skin`, `--wool`, `--night`, `--silver`, etc.): copy the values you need, don't import the doc's stylesheet. Page chrome (title, goal line, controls) in the doc's manuscript style: Google Fonts Cinzel (headings) and EB Garamond (text), parchment colors, with darker vellum under `prefers-color-scheme: dark`. The 3D scene itself stays dusk in both modes. A real `<title>` ("Lords & Logicians · Prototype"). Phone width: 16px side gutter, no horizontal page scroll.
  - **Board** (in `map.js`, as data so later levels or road-building are easy). Rows of characters: `.` grass, `#` road, `S` start (Hob's village end), `N` Lord Gainsay's tile (a road tile with his small gatehouse; he stands beside the road), `B` beacon tower (road's end), `t` decor (tree or rock). Starting map (the writer may tweak it but must keep one unbroken road S → N → B with bends):
    ```
    ..t.....t.
    S###......
    ...#..t...
    .t.N###...
    ......#.t.
    ..t...##B.
    ..........
    ```
    Tiles are separate slightly bevelled blocks with small gaps between them (grass tops, earthy sides). The road is a packed-earth strip inset on the tile top, shaped straight, corner or end from its road neighbours. `map.js` exports the ordered path (world positions of tile centres from S to B, found by following the road) and the tile positions of S, N and B.
  - **Play:** the player picks Hob's torch, **Lit (1)** or **Dark (0)** (the torch at the start updates at once), and presses **Send**. Hob walks the road to Lord Gainsay, stops on his tile, the lord snuffs a lit torch or lights a dark one, and **Hob himself continues** with the opposite torch to the beacon. The beacon lights only if he arrives lit. Goal line: "Light the beacon" (so the winning move is to send him dark). Readout of the run in truth-table terms, for example "In 1 lit → Lord Gainsay → Out 0 dark".

Hob walks on to B and raises his torch to the brazier: lit lights the beacon, with the message "The beacon burns over Contrary Keep!"; dark leaves it cold, with "The beacon stays dark. Try again." The readout fills in as the run goes ("In 1 lit → Lord Gainsay → Out 0 dark"). While running, the Lit/Dark choice and Send are disabled. Reset is always available: it puts Hob back on S with the chosen torch and puts the beacon out. After a run, Send starts a fresh run from S. An `aria-live` region announces the outcome. Done when both choices play through correctly, Reset works mid-run, and there are no console errors.
